## Configuration

<img src="/docsv2/image-v2/integrations/jira-1.png" alt="Jira Integration" width="450px" />

1. Install the Revolte app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
2. Connect your Revolte workspace to your Jira site.

<img src="/docsv2/image-v2/integrations/jira-2.png" alt="Jira Integration" width="450px" />

3. Map your Revolte projects to your Jira projects.

## Ticket Labeling

Revolte automatically tags linked Jira tickets with a specific label to identify the originating application:

`revolte-app=application name`

## Automatic Configuration

Revolte automatically detects linked Jira projects and applies default ticket labeling and status sync policies. You can further tune these in your project settings.

![Automatic Configuration](/images/automation_configuration.png)
