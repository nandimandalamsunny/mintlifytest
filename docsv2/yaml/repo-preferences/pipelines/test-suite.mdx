---
title: "Test Suite"
description: "Manage automated unit and integration testing."
---

The **Test Suite** component automates the execution of your project's tests. By integrating testing into your pipeline, you can ensure that new changes do not introduce regressions.

## Configuration

Enable the test suite in your `revolte.yaml` to trigger automated runs on every PR and promotion.

```yaml revolte.yaml
revolte:
  version: "1.0"
  project: Sideline HQ
  appName: sideline-web-admin

  config:
    pipeline:
      testSuite: true
```

## Testing Capabilities

- **Unit Tests**: Rapid validation of individual functions and components.
- **Integration Tests**: Verification of interactions between different parts of your system.
- **Coverage Reports**: (Optional) View the percentage of code exercised by your tests.
