## Overages & Billing

Usage beyond your plan limits is billed at the following PAYG (Pay-As-You-Go) rates:

- **AI Tokens**: $0.20 per 1K tokens
- **CI/CD Runs**: $0.01 per run
- **Log Ingestion**: $0.50 per GB

<Tip>
  Production usage is monitored by project and environment, allowing you to attribute spend accurately across your organization.
</Tip>

## Support Tiers

- **Free**: Community support & documentation.
- **Pro**: Email & Slack support (48h SLA) + Dedicated success manager.
- **Enterprise**: 4h priority support SLA + White-glove onboarding & migration.

## Monitoring Usage

You can track your consumption in real-time through the Revolte Dashboard to ensure your team remains within its plan limits.

### Usage Dashboard
Navigate to **Workspace Settings > Billing** to view:
- **Progress Trackers**: Real-time bars for CI/CD runs, AI token consumption, and log ingestion.
- **Service Quotas**: A breakdown of active Apps and Environments against your plan's maximum capacity.
- **Overage Estimates**: (Pro Only) Projected PAYG costs based on your current daily usage velocity.

### Quota Notifications
Revolte automatically sends email and in-app notifications at the following thresholds:
- **80% Usage**: A warning to help you plan for the remainder of the billing cycle.
- **100% Usage**: A critical alert. For Free plans, certain actions may be paused; for Pro plans, Pay-As-You-Go pricing will automatically apply to any additional usage.

<Tip>
  Team owners can configure **Budget Alerts** to receive notifications when projected monthly spend exceeds a custom dollar amount.
</Tip>

## How Limits are Enforced

Revolte provides clear feedback within the dashboard when you approach or reach your plan's capacity.

### Service & Environment Caps
If you reach your maximum number of active services (e.g., 3 services on the Free plan):
- **Dashboard Feedback**: The "Create Service" button will be disabled, and a **"Limit Reached"** badge will appear.
- **Resolution**: You can either delete an existing service to free up a slot or upgrade to the **Pro** plan for a higher capacity.

### CI/CD & Token Limits
For consumption-based limits like CI/CD runs or AI tokens:
- **Free Plan**: Workflows will be paused once the monthly quota is exhausted. They will automatically resume at the start of the next billing cycle.
- **Pro Plan**: Workflows continue uninterrupted. Any usage beyond the monthly quota is automatically billed at the Pay-As-You-Go overage rates.
