## 2026 Strategy

Our focus for the coming year is to bridge the gap between code generation and operational health.

### Q2 - Intelligence & Review
We are deepening the specialized knowledge of our agent fleet.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The Agent Expansion" icon="robot">
    Following our BA and Code Review agents, we are introducing **QA**, **DevOps**, and **Security** agents. These specialists will work in parallel to provide a 360-degree automated review of every change before it reaches a human.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automated Documentation" icon="book-sparkles">
    The **Documentation Agent** will automatically keep your internal guides and API references in sync with implementation changes, ensuring your docs never rot.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Q3 - AI-Powered Operations
We are bringing the same intelligence that builds your code to how you monitor it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI Log Monitoring" icon="monitor-waveform">
    The upcoming **AI Monitor** will analyze log streams in real-time. It doesn't just detect errors; it explains them in plain language and prepares implementation fixes for identified bugs automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multiple Dashboards" icon="browsers">
    Customizable views for different organizational needs. Create specialized dashboards for **Security Compliance**, **Deployment Velocity**, or **System Health** across multiple projects.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Long-term Vision

<Note title="Self-Healing Delivery Loop">
  Our ultimate goal is a system where Revolte detects a production failure, identifies the cause through log analysis, and prepares a verified fix pull request—all before your team starts their morning standup.
</Note>
