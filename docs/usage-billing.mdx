Usage and billing gives administrators a single place to understand how teams consume Revolte across agent orchestration, build workflows, validation, and deployment activity. Use it to monitor consumption, manage limits, and support internal governance.

## Before you start

You need:

- Administrator or billing access to the workspace
- The workspace or organization you want to review
- Agreement on who owns budget review and usage governance
- Any internal reporting period, cost center, or project mapping your finance process requires

## Usage Overview

Platform usage in Revolte is measured by the execution of operational workflows and the orchestration of specialized AI agents. Every action taken to plan, implement, or verify a change contributes to your organization's consumption.

Key activities that contribute to platform usage include:

- **AI orchestration**: Reasoning cycles executed by specialized agents such as BA, Code Review, and QA agents.
- **Build generation**: Repository analysis and implementation work required to produce pull requests.
- **Deployment workflows**: Release orchestration, environment initialization, and deployment verification.
- **Validation pipelines**: Automated quality gates, linting, and test-suite execution.

## Workflow Consumption

Usage is attributed directly to specific engineering workflows, providing teams with the granularity needed to optimize operational activity at scale. This attribution allows organizations to:

- **Identify high-impact workflows**: Understand which projects or applications consume the most platform resources.
- **Optimize consumption**: Review workflow execution patterns to reduce redundant or inefficient activity.
- **Plan for growth**: Track how usage changes with team size, repository complexity, and delivery volume.

## Usage Credits & Limits

To simplify operational management, Revolte uses a credit-based system for platform consumption. Organizations are allocated credits that are consumed as workflows are executed by agents and deployment systems.

### Allocation & Thresholds
Administrators can configure organizational limits to prevent unexpected consumption spikes. Revolte provides automated notifications when consumption reaches specific thresholds (e.g., 80% and 95% of allocated capacity), allowing for proactive resource management without interrupting engineering velocity.

### Resource Governance
Credits are consumed based on the complexity and duration of the operational activity. A simple documentation update will consume fewer resources than a complex, multi-repository architectural refactor involving multiple specialized agents and validation cycles.

## Billing Visibility

Revolte provides enterprise-oriented billing dashboards that offer a single source of truth for all operational costs.

- **Centralized tracking**: Monitor usage trends across multiple teams and projects from one administrative view.
- **Consumption forecasting**: Use historical data to plan future resource needs and budget expectations.
- **Audit-ready reporting**: Export detailed usage reports for internal accounting and engineering governance reviews.
