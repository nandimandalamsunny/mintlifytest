## Dashboard Connection Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Application">
    Select your application, navigate to the **Overview** tab, and click the **Connect** button in
    the top-right corner.
    <img src="/docs/image-v2/deployments/db/db-2.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch Data">
    Write your query and click **Run** to fetch live data.
    <img src="/docs/image-v2/deployments/db/db-3.png" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Queries">
    Save your queries for future use.
    <div align="center">
      <img src="/docs/image-v2/deployments/db/db-4.png" width="300" />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Saved Queries">
    Review and manage your previously saved queries.
    <img src="/docs/image-v2/deployments/db/db-5.png" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
