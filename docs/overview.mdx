Revolte is an AI-native software delivery platform that helps engineering teams move from intent to production through one connected execution system.
 It brings planning, code generation, validation, pull request review, deployment, and observability into a governed workflow where AI executes the work and teams remain in control.

In many organizations, engineering leaders already have strong teams, modern tools, and capable developers. What is often missing is not effort, but a connected operating layer that turns ideas, tickets, code, reviews, environments, deployments, and runtime feedback into one reliable delivery motion. Revolte is designed to become that layer.

## What you can do with Revolte

- **From Intent to Delivery.** Convert requirements, tickets, deployments, and operational requests into executable AI SDLC workflows across the delivery lifecycle.

- **Context-Aware AI Execution.** Continuously enrich AI workflows using repositories, runtime telemetry, infrastructure state, organizational workflows, and operational context.

- **AI Systems Across the SDLC.** Coordinate specialized, custom, and marketplace AI agents across implementation, validation, testing, deployment, operations, and organization-specific engineering workflows.

- **Workflow-Driven Software Delivery.** Shape customizable AI SDLC workflows around how your organization plans, builds, validates, deploys, and operates software.

- **Beyond Traditional CI/CD.** Connect validation, preview environments, deployment orchestration, rollback coordination, runtime awareness, and operational visibility into one governed execution workflow.

- **Continuous Delivery Intelligence.** Continuously connect runtime telemetry, deployment performance, observability, and operational feedback back into the software delivery workflow.
