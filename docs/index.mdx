## What you can do with Revolte

- **Describe the outcome, not the implementation.** Revolte's AI agents turn high-level intent into production-ready code, tests, and configuration.
- **Ship with built-in safety.** Every change is automatically validated and reviewed before it ever reaches your production environment.
- **Automate your entire SDLC.** From repository setup and CI/CD to environment management and DORA metrics—everything is handled in one place.
- **Scale without DevOps bottlenecks.** Revolte manages the underlying infrastructure so your team can focus on building features, not pipelines.

## How Revolte works

1. **Describe your Intent**: Submit requirements in natural language through the Dashboard or CLI.
2. **AI Agents Execute**: Specialized agents generate your code, tests, and deployment configurations.
3. **Review & Iterate**: Preview your changes instantly in managed environments and perform AI-assisted reviews.
4. **Deploy & Scale**: Promote to production with confidence and monitor performance natively.
