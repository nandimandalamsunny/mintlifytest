The Revolte Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of your software delivery lifecycle through **DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment)** metrics. These metrics help teams measure their velocity, stability, and overall effectiveness.

## Key Metrics

Revolte automatically tracks the four key DORA metrics across your production services.

### 1. Deployment Frequency
**Measure of Velocity**
Track how often your team successfully releases to production. High-performing teams aim for frequent, smaller deployments to reduce risk and deliver value faster.

### 2. Lead Time for Changes
**Measure of Efficiency**
Calculate the time it takes for a commit to reach production. This metric highlights bottlenecks in your development and deployment pipeline.

### 3. Change Failure Rate
**Measure of Stability**
The percentage of deployments that cause a failure in production. A lower rate indicates more stable releases and robust testing processes.

### 4. Time to Restore Service
**Measure of Recovery**
How long it takes to recover from a failure in production. This metric reflects your team's ability to respond to incidents and restore service quickly.

---

## Using the Dashboard

Access the DORA Dashboard from your Revolte workspace to:

- **Filter by Service**: View metrics for individual web applications or services.
- **Time Range Selection**: Compare performance across weeks, months, or custom quarters.
- **Trend Analysis**: Identify whether your delivery performance is improving or declining over time.

<Tip>
  Consistent monitoring of DORA metrics is the first step toward building a high-performing engineering culture. Use these insights to drive process improvements and automate bottlenecks.
</Tip>
