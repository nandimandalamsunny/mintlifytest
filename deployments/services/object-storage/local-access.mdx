title: "Local Access & Credentials"

---

## Dashboard guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Service">
    Navigate to your application, go to the **Services** tab, and select your **Storage Bucket**
    service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Credentials">
    Switch to the **Credentials** tab and click **Generate New Key** to create your access
    credentials.
    <div align="center">
      <img src="/image-v2/deployments/storage-buckets/sb-4.png" width="300" />
    </div>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Access Keys">
    Review, rotate, or revoke your active access keys from the credentials management list.
    <div align="center">
      <img src="/image-v2/deployments/storage-buckets/sb-5.png" />
    </div>
  </Step>
</Steps>
